# 人物像カード画像：生成プロンプト

全26点は、画像生成の組み込みモードで新規作成した。

## 共通プロンプト

> Landscape persona card image for a Japanese wisdom and social-skills app. Warm editorial photography, understated contemporary Japanese sensibility, realistic textures, subtle film grain, horizontal 3:2 composition, one clear symbolic idea, soft warm natural light, cream/walnut/charcoal/muted-gold palette. No text, logos, watermark, excessive gloss, fantasy effects, or uncanny faces. Compose for responsive card cropping.
